Golf Cart Rentals in Port Aransas

Rental companies, pricing, driving rules, and tips for getting around Mustang Island

Published on November 16, 2026

Renting a golf cart is one of the most popular ways to get around Port Aransas. The town is small and flat, the speed limits are low, and a golf cart gives you an easy way to cruise between the beach, restaurants, and shops without dealing with parking. Most visitors rent one for a full day or the duration of their stay, and several local companies offer 4-seat and 6-seat carts with delivery to your rental property. Here is what you need to know before you book.

Where to Rent a Golf Cart

Port Aransas has several golf cart rental companies, each with slightly different pricing, cart sizes, and policies. Here are the most established options.

Bron's Beach Carts offers 4-passenger walk-up carts and 6-passenger carts that can be reserved online. Their posted off-season rates (September through February) start around $100 plus tax for a 4-seat cart and $124 plus tax for a 6-seat cart, both for 24 hours. Peak season rates are higher. Cancellations made at least 24 hours ahead are eligible for a refund.

Jackfish Cart Rentals handles online booking through FareHarbor and operates a shop at 3411 South 11th Street. They offer themed carts on a first-come, first-served basis. Their cancellation policy includes a 25% deduction for cancellations at least 48 hours in advance.

Marlins Beach Carts rents 4-seat and 6-seat carts with online or phone reservations. Delivery and pickup within Port Aransas costs $60, with no delivery past Beach Mile Marker 45, Corpus Christi, or Aransas Pass. No deposit is required, but a credit card must be on file.

Island Surf Rentals offers 4-passenger and 6-passenger carts. Their rentals include a City license plate and beach permit.

Mustang Island Adventures is listed as Cinnamon Shore's preferred on-site provider. They offer complimentary home delivery and complimentary gas with their rentals, which is a convenience if you are staying in the Cinnamon Shore community.

Tip: Posted rates are not always all-in prices. Before you book, confirm the total cost including taxes, mandatory fees, delivery charges, late-return fees, deposits, and any authorization holds on your card. Get everything in writing.

How Much Does a Golf Cart Rental Cost?

Pricing varies by company, cart size, season, and rental duration. As a rough guide, expect to pay somewhere between $100 and $200 per day for a 4-seat cart and $125 to $250 per day for a 6-seat cart, depending on the time of year and the provider. Peak season (summer and spring break) rates are significantly higher than off-season pricing.

Multi-day rentals usually come with a lower per-day rate. If you are staying for a full week, ask each provider for their weekly rate since the savings can be substantial. Always request a full quote with all fees included before committing, since advertised starting prices do not always reflect the final total.

Delivery and Pickup

Most rental companies offer delivery to vacation homes and condos within Port Aransas. Some charge a delivery fee (Marlins charges $60, for example), while others like Mustang Island Adventures include delivery for free. A few things to confirm before you pay:

Make sure the company delivers to your exact address. Delivery to every property on Mustang Island is not guaranteed, especially for addresses farther from the main town area. Get written confirmation of your delivery address, the arrival window, and the return time so there are no surprises on either end.

Driving Rules in Port Aransas

Golf carts are street-legal in Port Aransas, but they are subject to real traffic laws. The City treats carts the same as any other vehicle on the road, which means the following rules apply:

A valid driver's license or operator's permit is required. Seat belt laws apply to all passengers. Open-container and DWI laws apply just like they do in a car. Every passenger must be in a designated seat. No one should ride in another person's lap, stand on the back, or sit on the armrests.

The beach is treated as a city street, so the same rules apply when driving on the sand. Golf carts are prohibited on TX-361 (State Highway 361) south of Avenue G. This is the main highway that runs along Mustang Island, and it has speed limits and traffic that are not safe for golf carts.

Sidewalks, parks, and playgrounds are off-limits. A pedestrian crosswalk does not automatically authorize golf cart use. When in doubt about a specific route, check with the City of Port Aransas directly or ask your rental company for guidance.

Tip: If you are staying at Cinnamon Shore and want to drive into the main town area, do not take TX-361. The highway restriction means you will need to use the community's internal roads and side streets to reach Avenue G and the downtown area. Ask your rental company or the Cinnamon Shore front desk for the best route.

Golf Carts at Cinnamon Shore

The Cinnamon Shore community maintains an approved-vendor list for golf cart rentals. Using a cart from an unapproved vendor may result in the cart being towed. Before you book, check with Cinnamon Shore's property management to confirm that your chosen rental company is on the approved list.

Keep in mind that vendor approval, community road access, and delivery confirmation to your specific property are three separate things. Confirm all three before your trip to avoid any issues on arrival day.

Other Ways to Get Around

Golf carts are the most popular option, but Port Aransas has other ways to explore the island depending on your style and group size.

Bike rentals are available for those who want to pedal around town. The terrain is flat, which makes cycling easy, and bike-friendly paths connect many of the main areas.

Jeep rentals are a good option if you want to drive on the beach farther down Mustang Island, since golf carts have limited range and speed for longer beach drives. A beach parking permit is required for driving any vehicle on the sand.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to rent a golf cart in Port Aransas?
Rates vary by company, season, and cart size. Expect roughly $100 to $200 per day for a 4-seat cart and $125 to $250 per day for a 6-seat cart. Always request a full quote with all fees included.

Do I need a driver's license to operate a golf cart?
Yes. A valid driver's license or operator's permit is required. All standard traffic laws apply, including seat belt, open-container, and DWI regulations.

Can I drive a golf cart on the beach?
Yes. The beach is treated as a city street in Port Aransas, so golf carts are allowed. The same driving rules apply on the sand as they do on the road.

Can I drive a golf cart on Highway 361?
No. Golf carts are prohibited on TX-361 south of Avenue G. Use side streets and internal community roads instead.

Will the rental company deliver to my vacation home?
Most companies offer delivery within Port Aransas, but not every address is covered. Confirm delivery to your exact address in writing before you book.

How many seats do I need?
Choose a cart with enough designated seats for your entire group. Every passenger must have their own seat. Riding on laps, armrests, or standing on the back is not allowed.
