Closest Airport to Port Aransas

Every airport option for getting to Mustang Island, from regional hubs to major Texas airports

Corpus Christi International Airport (CRP) is the closest commercial airport to Port Aransas, sitting about 35 to 40 minutes south of Mustang Island by car. It is a small regional airport with service from American, United, and Southwest, and for many travelers it is the fastest way to get from a plane to the beach.

But closest does not always mean best. Depending on where you are flying from, what airlines serve your home airport, and how the fares compare, flying into San Antonio, Austin, or Houston can sometimes make more sense. This guide walks through every option so you can figure out which airport works best for your trip to Port Aransas.

Corpus Christi International Airport (CRP)

CRP is the default choice for most visitors. It is a small, easy-to-navigate airport with three commercial carriers: American Airlines, United Airlines, and Southwest Airlines. You will not find budget carriers like Frontier, Spirit, or Allegiant here, which means fares can run higher than what you would pay into a larger Texas hub.

The upside is convenience. The terminal is compact, bag claim is quick, and the rental car counters are inside the building. From the airport, the drive to Cinnamon Shore on Mustang Island takes roughly 35 to 40 minutes using the JFK Causeway route.

Tip: Book rental cars early if you are visiting during spring break, summer weekends, or large Corpus Christi events. The fleet at CRP is smaller than what you would find at a major hub, and availability can get tight during peak periods.

Rental Cars at CRP

The rental car brands available at CRP include Enterprise, Hertz, Avis, National, Budget, and Alamo. Pickup is in the terminal, which makes the process quick. That said, rates at CRP can sometimes run higher than what you would pay at San Antonio or Austin for the same vehicle class. If the price difference is large, it may be worth considering a longer drive from a bigger airport where rental rates are more competitive.

Rideshare and Shuttles from CRP

Uber and Lyft both operate at Corpus Christi International. Walk-up taxis are limited, so if you are not renting a car, plan on using a rideshare app or booking a private shuttle in advance.

There is no scheduled shuttle service from CRP to Port Aransas. Private on-demand shuttle services run roughly $80 to $120 one way. If you are heading back to CRP for your return flight, book your morning rideshare the night before. Drivers tend to cluster in the Corpus Christi area, and wait times from Port Aransas can be unpredictable if you are requesting a ride on the spot.

Parking at CRP

If someone is picking you up at the airport, CRP has a free cell-phone waiting lot signed at the airport entrance. The driver waits there until you call or text, then pulls up to the terminal. Short-term, hourly, and economy parking lots are also available steps from the terminal for travelers leaving a vehicle while they fly.

Driving from CRP to Port Aransas

The route from CRP to Port Aransas avoids the ferry entirely if you use the JFK Causeway approach. Take TX-358 (South Padre Island Drive) east across the JFK Causeway onto North Padre Island, then follow TX-361 north up Mustang Island into Port Aransas.

This is a straightforward drive with no tolls. The causeway bridge gives you a nice view of the Laguna Madre as you cross onto the island. For a full breakdown of route options, including the ferry from Aransas Pass, see the directions to Port Aransas guide.

San Antonio International Airport (SAT)

San Antonio International is the next closest major airport. The drive from SAT to Port Aransas takes roughly 2.5 to 3 hours, depending on traffic through the city and along I-37 south.

The big advantage of SAT is airline selection. It is served by American, Delta, United, Southwest, Frontier, Spirit, and Alaska Airlines. That means more nonstop routes from more cities and significantly lower fares on budget carriers. If a Frontier or Spirit fare into San Antonio saves you $200 or more per person compared to flying into CRP, the extra driving time can easily be worth it.

Rental car options at SAT are extensive, and rates tend to be more competitive than CRP. From the airport, take I-37 south toward Corpus Christi, then follow TX-358 east across the JFK Causeway onto Mustang Island.

Austin-Bergstrom International Airport (AUS)

Austin-Bergstrom is a full-service airport with every major carrier plus budget options like Frontier, Spirit, Allegiant, and Sun Country. It is a popular choice for travelers coming from the West Coast, Midwest, and East Coast who cannot find a reasonable connection into CRP.

The drive from AUS to Port Aransas runs about 3.5 to 4 hours. The route takes you south on I-35 through San Marcos and San Antonio, then I-37 south to Corpus Christi and across the causeway. If you are leaving Austin during a weekday afternoon, pad extra time for traffic on I-35 through the city. The SH-130 toll bypass east of town can save 30 to 45 minutes when traffic is heavy.

For travelers who can find a cheap nonstop into Austin, this airport is worth considering. The fare savings on budget carriers can more than offset the extra drive time, especially for groups splitting fuel and rental costs.

AUS or SAT? If both airports have similar fares from your departure city, SAT is the better pick because the drive is shorter. Choose AUS when it has a significantly cheaper or more convenient nonstop flight that SAT does not.

Houston Airports (HOU and IAH)

Houston has two commercial airports, and both are roughly 3.5 to 4 hours from Port Aransas by car.

Houston Hobby (HOU) is served primarily by Southwest and Delta. It is a smaller, faster airport to get in and out of compared to IAH. If you are flying Southwest, Hobby is usually your Houston option.

Houston George Bush Intercontinental (IAH) is United's major hub and serves all major domestic carriers plus international flights. It is the largest airport in the Houston area and can take longer to navigate, especially during connections. That said, it has the widest selection of nonstop domestic and international routes of any Texas airport.

From either Houston airport, the drive follows US-59 south to Victoria, then US-77 and TX-35 south to Aransas Pass, where you can take the free ferry to Port Aransas. Alternatively, continue south to Corpus Christi and use the JFK Causeway for a ferry-free route. For a detailed look at this drive, see the closest beach to Houston guide.

Mustang Beach Airport (KRAS)

Mustang Beach Airport is technically the closest airport to Port Aransas because it is in Port Aransas. However, it handles only general aviation and private or charter flights. There is no commercial airline service here.

If you have access to a private aircraft or want to charter a flight, KRAS puts you on the island itself. For everyone else, CRP is the closest option with scheduled airline service.

How to Choose the Right Airport

The cheapest flight is not always the best deal. When you are comparing airports, factor in the total trip cost and time, not just the airfare.

Compare total travel time. A nonstop flight into a larger hub often beats a one-stop connection into CRP, even with a longer drive at the end. Add up your flight time, layover, bag claim, rental car pickup, and drive to Port Aransas for each option before deciding.

Compare total cost. Airfare plus rental car plus fuel. CRP is closest, but if the rental rate is $30 a day higher than SAT and the flight is $150 more, the savings at a bigger airport can add up fast.

Consider your group size. For a family of four or a group trip, the per-person savings from a budget carrier into AUS or SAT can be substantial. Those savings often more than cover the extra fuel for a longer drive.

Check the nonstops. A nonstop flight from your city into SAT, AUS, or HOU is almost always better than a connecting flight through Dallas or Houston into CRP. The connection adds hours, and the risk of delays or missed connections is real, especially during summer storm season on the Gulf Coast.

Tip: Run the same departure date through Google Flights for CRP, SAT, AUS, and HOU all at once. Sort by total travel time, then compare fares. The best option is usually obvious once you see all four side by side.

What to Do Once You Arrive

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the closest airport to Port Aransas?
Corpus Christi International Airport (CRP) is the closest commercial airport, about 35 to 40 minutes south of Mustang Island. Mustang Beach Airport (KRAS) is in Port Aransas itself but handles only private and charter flights.

What is the closest airport to Cinnamon Shore?
CRP is the closest commercial airport to Cinnamon Shore. The drive from the airport to the community takes roughly 35 to 40 minutes using the JFK Causeway route.

What airlines fly into Corpus Christi?
American Airlines, United Airlines, and Southwest Airlines serve CRP. Budget carriers like Frontier, Spirit, and Allegiant do not fly into Corpus Christi.

Should I fly into San Antonio or Austin instead?
If SAT or AUS have significantly cheaper fares or better nonstop options from your city, the longer drive can be worth it. SAT is about 2.5 to 3 hours from Port Aransas; AUS is about 3.5 to 4 hours. Both airports have more airline options and often better rental car rates.

Is there an airport shuttle from CRP to Port Aransas?
There is no scheduled shuttle service. Private on-demand shuttles run roughly $80 to $120 one way. Uber and Lyft also operate at CRP, but pre-book your return ride from Port Aransas the night before since drivers tend to cluster in the Corpus Christi area.

Can I get an Uber or Lyft from CRP?
Yes. Both Uber and Lyft operate at Corpus Christi International. Walk-up taxi availability is limited, so the rideshare apps are the more reliable option if you are not renting a car.

What rental car companies are at CRP?
Enterprise, Hertz, Avis, National, Budget, and Alamo all have counters inside the terminal. Book early during spring break and summer weekends when the smaller fleet can run low on availability.

Do I need a rental car in Port Aransas?
It depends on your plans. The town itself is walkable and golf-cart friendly. But if you want to explore beyond the island with day trips to Padre Island National Seashore or the Aransas Wildlife Refuge, having your own vehicle is a big advantage.

Is there free parking at CRP for someone picking me up?
Yes. CRP has a free cell-phone waiting lot at the airport entrance. Your driver waits there until you call or text that you are ready, then pulls up to the terminal curb.
