Best Fishing Spots in Port Aransas

A local guide to the top places to cast a line on Mustang Island

Port Aransas is one of the most popular fishing destinations on the Texas Gulf Coast, and for good reason. Whether you prefer wading into the surf, casting from the jetties, or heading offshore on a deep-sea charter, this small island town delivers some of the best saltwater fishing in the state. Here is a breakdown of the top spots and what you can expect at each one.

The South Jetty

The South Jetty is one of the most iconic fishing spots in Port Aransas. Located at the northern tip of Mustang Island where the ship channel meets the Gulf, the jetty attracts a wide variety of species year-round. Redfish, speckled trout, sheepshead, and black drum are all common catches here. During the warmer months, you can also hook tarpon, jack crevalle, and the occasional kingfish.

The rocks can be slippery, so wear shoes with good grip and be mindful of the waves. Early morning and late afternoon tend to produce the best results, especially during tidal changes.

Horace Caldwell Pier

If you want a more accessible fishing experience, the Horace Caldwell Pier is a great choice. This pier extends into the Gulf and is open to the public for a small fee. It is a family-friendly spot where you can fish without a boat or any advanced gear. Whiting, pompano, and sand trout are common catches from the pier, and you will occasionally see someone pull in a nice-sized shark or red drum.

Tip: You can rent rods and buy bait at the pier shop, so even if you did not bring your own gear, you can still have a great day of fishing.

Lydia Ann Channel

The Lydia Ann Channel is a favorite among experienced anglers and locals. This deep channel runs between San Jose Island and Harbor Island, and the strong currents bring in plenty of fish. Redfish, flounder, and speckled trout are the primary targets here, and the channel is especially productive during fall and winter months. You will need a boat to fish this area, but several local marinas offer rentals and guided trips.

Surf Fishing on Mustang Island

You do not need a boat or a pier to catch fish in Port Aransas. Surf fishing along the beach is a popular and rewarding option. Drive your vehicle onto the beach (a permit is required), set up your rod holders in the sand, and cast into the first or second sandbar. Pompano, whiting, redfish, and the occasional bull drum are all possible catches. Fall is generally the best season for surf fishing, when migrating fish move through the area in large numbers.

Deep-Sea Fishing Charters

For those looking for a bigger adventure, Port Aransas has a fleet of offshore charter boats that head out into the Gulf of Mexico. Half-day and full-day trips are available, targeting species like red snapper (in season), king mackerel, mahi-mahi, and amberjack. The deep-sea fleet operates out of the marina near the ferry landing, and most charter companies include all the gear, bait, and a fish-cleaning service at the end of the trip.

What You Need to Know

A Texas saltwater fishing license is required for anyone 17 and older. You can purchase one online through the Texas Parks and Wildlife Department website or at most local tackle shops. Bag limits and size restrictions vary by species and season, so check the current regulations before you head out. Live bait like shrimp and mullet can be purchased at several bait shops around town, and most open early in the morning for your convenience.

No matter your skill level, Port Aransas offers a fishing experience that is hard to beat on the Texas coast. Grab your gear, pick a spot, and enjoy the salt air.
