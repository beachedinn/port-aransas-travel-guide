Best Restaurants in Port Aransas

Waterfront seafood, walkable Cinnamon Shore dining, beach-town classics, and more on Mustang Island

Published on November 18, 2026

Port Aransas has a dining scene that punches well above its weight for a town of roughly 4,000 year-round residents. The options range from waterfront fine dining and cook-your-catch spots on the harbor to walkable village restaurants in Cinnamon Shore, family-friendly pizza joints, Cajun seafood boils, and early-morning taco shops that have been feeding locals since before the tourist boom. Here is a neighborhood-by-neighborhood breakdown of where to eat, what to expect, and a few things to know before you go.

Cinnamon Shore Restaurants

If you are staying at Beached Inn or anywhere in the Cinnamon Shore community, the village has a full lineup of restaurants within walking distance. No car or golf cart needed for dinner.

Lisabella's is the village's sit-down dinner restaurant, offering a full menu in a relaxed coastal setting. It is the go-to option for a proper dinner without leaving the community.

Stout's at the Shore, located in the North Town Center, is an upscale New American bistro with a land-and-sea menu and craft cocktails. It is dinner-only and reservations are recommended, especially on weekends and during peak season.

Tiki Jay's, in the Nautilus building at Cinnamon Shore North, serves Texas-style poke bowls and tropical cocktails in a tiki bar setting. It is a solid pick for a casual lunch or early evening drinks.

Dylan's Pizzeria is the family-friendly pizza option in the village. Good for a quick dinner with kids or a takeout order back to the house.

Shore Cafe on Market Street is open from 7 AM to 9 PM and covers breakfast, lunch, and evening. Morning espresso and pastries, lunchtime paninis, and a wine-and-beer lounge in the evening. They also offer pre-orderable charcuterie boards if you want something ready when you arrive.

Coastline Grill is a poolside bar and grill at the South pool complex, open seasonally from mid-May through Labor Day, 11 AM to 8 PM daily. Full bar with QR-code mobile ordering so you do not have to leave your chair.

C Bar at Keira's Pool is another seasonal poolside option, also running mid-May through Labor Day, with a full bar and light meals.

C Treats is the sweets spot for ice cream and after-beach treats.

Dos Bocas is a new coastal Mexican and Cajun fusion restaurant overlooking South Town Lake. It has a grand opening planned for late fall 2026, ahead of the 2027 season.

Tip: For a walkable evening in Cinnamon Shore, start with drinks at Tiki Jay's, move to dinner at Stout's or Lisabella's, and finish with something sweet at C Treats. No driving required.

Waterfront Seafood and Fine Dining

Port Aransas has several waterfront spots where the views are as much a draw as the food. Most of these are about 12 minutes from Cinnamon Shore by car or golf cart.

FINS Grill and Icehouse at 420 W. Cotter Avenue is a harbor-front restaurant known for its cook-your-catch program. Bring in your fresh catch from a fishing charter and they will prepare it for you. No reservations; seating is first-come, first-served, and the full party must be present to be seated.

Virginia's on the Bay is a two-story open-air harborfront restaurant that has been operating since 1996. They also run a dock-and-dine program and a bring-your-own-fish option. It is a Port Aransas institution and one of the best waterfront dining experiences in town.

Trout Street Bar and Grill sits marina-side with Gulf views, oysters, and a full seafood menu. Another solid option for a waterfront lunch or dinner.

Roosevelt's at the Tarpon Inn is dinner-only fine dining inside the Historic Tarpon Inn, which dates back to 1886. Reservations are accepted, and the dress code is smart-casual. This is probably the most upscale restaurant in Port Aransas proper.

Venetian Hot Plate on Beach Avenue serves North Italian fine dining and has been open since 1995. Dinner only, Tuesday through Saturday. Reservations are mandatory, and cash is preferred.

The Phoenix Restaurant and Bar at Island Moorings Marina is an Asian-fusion fine dining spot that opened in 2018. Open Wednesday through Sunday for dinner. The sunset deck overlooks the lagoon, and the seasonal Triple Tail is a popular order.

Black Marlin Bar and Grill at Palmilla Beach Resort serves coastal and Southern comfort food with a third-floor open-air bar overlooking the golf course. About five minutes from Cinnamon Shore by golf cart.

Casual Eats and Beach-Town Classics

These are the spots that give Port Aransas its laid-back character. Most are casual, most are affordable, and several have been around for decades.

Grumbles Seafood Co. at 850 Tarpon Street on The Wharf is fast-casual Gulf seafood with a covered deck. No reservations needed. It is one of the most popular lunch spots in town for good reason.

Tortuga's Saltwater Grill at 429 N. Alister Street serves dinner, weekend brunch, and bar menus. They offer online table booking, which makes it one of the easier sit-down restaurants to plan around during busy weekends.

Bierhaus PortA at 522 East Avenue G is a beer-garden-style spot with burgers, bratwursts, pretzels, and a solid beer selection. The outdoor patio is dog-friendly.

The Gaff on Beach Street is a pirate-themed dive bar with scratch-made pizza. It is known for its Belt Sander Races, which is exactly what it sounds like. Good for a casual night out.

MacDaddy's Family Kitchen on Beach Street serves Texas pit barbecue and Southern comfort food. Built for big groups, with a pet-friendly deck and Thursday trivia nights.

Port A Beachlodge at Beach Marker 9 is an open-air beachfront bar and grill that has been around since 1969. The kitchen runs all day from 8 AM to 10 PM, and parking is free. The pet-friendly deck is a plus if you are traveling with a dog.

Seafood and Spaghetti Works on Highway 361 serves Italian and Gulf seafood under a 1979 geodesic dome. All-you-can-eat salad bar, walk-up seating only.

The Crazy Cajun is a family-style Cajun seafood boil restaurant. Bibs are included. This is the messy, hands-on seafood experience.

Bron's Shaved Ice and To Go Bar in Old Town on East Avenue G is a shaved ice and frozen cocktail spot with a live-music stage, lawn games, and a dog-friendly setup.

Rollin' Tide Boil Co. does something different. They deliver a mobile shrimp boil directly to the beach for a bonfire setup. BYOB, and advance booking is required. See the things to do in Port Aransas guide for more on beach bonfires and activities.

Tip: Port Aransas is a fishing town. When you sit down at any seafood restaurant, ask about the day's catch. The fresh Gulf fish specials are almost always the best thing on the menu.

Mexican and Tex-Mex

La Playa Mexican Grille is a dinner-only coastal Mexican restaurant with over 70 tequilas. Cash only, so plan accordingly.

San Juan Restaurant on Cut-Off Road has been open since 2004 and starts serving at 6 AM daily. This is where locals go for breakfast tacos, carne guisada, and fresh fish tacos through their bring-your-own-fish program. Weekend menudo is available for those who know to ask for it.

Breakfast, Coffee, and Sweets

Port Aransas has a handful of solid morning spots beyond the Cinnamon Shore options.

Coffee Waves is the island's longest-running independent coffee shop. Good coffee, no frills.

Island Market Smoothie and Juice Bar on Gulf Street serves acai bowls, lox bagels, avocado toast, savory tacos, and specialty coffee. It covers both the healthy-breakfast crowd and the coffee-first crowd.

Port A Donut Shop has donuts, kolaches, and breakfast tacos. Simple and consistent.

Coach's Island Grill is a diner that serves breakfast and lunch only. Doors close by 2 PM, no dinner service. Get there early if you want a table on a busy morning.

For a broader look at coffee and dessert options, see the coffee shops and ice cream and snow cones guides on the Beached Inn site.

Day Trip Dining

If you are heading toward Corpus Christi or Padre Island for a day trip, two restaurants along the JFK Causeway are worth a stop. Snoopy's Pier is a classic Texas Gulf seafood spot, and Doc's Seafood and Steaks is a waterfront option with a full menu. Both are about 30 minutes from Cinnamon Shore.

Tips for Dining in Port Aransas

Call ahead about large groups, kids' menus, allergies, or dietary restrictions. Not every restaurant handles these the same way, and a quick phone call saves you a problem at the door.

Outdoor seating depends on weather, and specific tables or pet access are not always guaranteed. If a patio table matters to you, arrive early or confirm the policy in advance.

Hours and menus change with the season. Summer service often runs later, and some spots reduce hours or close entirely during the off-season winter months. Check directly before making the drive.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best restaurants in Port Aransas?
It depends on what you are looking for. For waterfront seafood, FINS Grill and Virginia's on the Bay are top picks. For fine dining, Roosevelt's at the Tarpon Inn and Venetian Hot Plate stand out. For casual eats, Grumbles and Bierhaus are popular. If you are staying at Cinnamon Shore, Stout's at the Shore and Lisabella's are walkable dinner options.

Are there restaurants within walking distance of Cinnamon Shore?
Yes. Cinnamon Shore has nine dining options in the village, including Lisabella's, Stout's at the Shore, Tiki Jay's, Dylan's Pizzeria, Shore Cafe, Coastline Grill, C Bar, C Treats, and the upcoming Dos Bocas.

Do I need reservations?
For Stout's at the Shore, Roosevelt's, Venetian Hot Plate, and The Phoenix, reservations are recommended or required. Most casual spots are walk-up or first-come seating.

Which restaurants offer cook-your-catch?
FINS Grill and Icehouse, Virginia's on the Bay, and San Juan Restaurant all accept fresh-caught fish. Bring your cleaned catch from a fishing trip and they will cook it for you.

Where should I go for breakfast?
Shore Cafe at Cinnamon Shore opens at 7 AM. In town, San Juan Restaurant opens at 6 AM for breakfast tacos, Coffee Waves has the best independent coffee, and Coach's Island Grill serves diner breakfast until 2 PM.

Are any restaurants dog-friendly?
Bierhaus PortA, MacDaddy's, Port A Beachlodge, and Bron's all have pet-friendly outdoor areas. Always confirm current policies before bringing your dog.
