Best Time to Visit Port Aransas

A month-by-month guide to weather, crowds, pricing, and what to do each season

Home

Published on November 12, 2026

Port Aransas is a year-round destination, but when you visit makes a real difference in what your trip looks like. Water temperature, crowd levels, rental pricing, and the activities available all shift from month to month. The short answer is that late April through early June hits the sweet spot of warm water, manageable crowds, and moderate pricing. But every season has something worth showing up for. Here is a full breakdown so you can pick the window that fits what you are looking for.

Quick picks: Best overall is late April to early June. Warmest swimming is July and August. Calmest weather and best birding is October and November. Lowest prices and emptiest beaches are December through February.

January and February

Winter on Mustang Island is mild compared to most of the country. January highs average around 64 degrees and February warms up to about 67. The Gulf water sits around 60 degrees, which is too cold for most swimmers but fine for beachcombing, fishing, and exploring. Crowds are low and rental pricing is at its most affordable.

This is prime season for birding. The endangered whooping cranes are at the Aransas National Wildlife Refuge from November through March, and winter is when the crane boat tours run from Rockport. The Whooping Crane Festival in Rockport typically falls in February and draws birders from across the country. If you are a fisherman, winter produces good redfish and black drum action in the bays.

The main caution is cold fronts. Temperatures can drop sharply for a few days when a norther blows through, so pack layers and check the forecast before heading out.

March

March is the busiest month of the year in Port Aransas because of Texas Spring Break. Highs reach the low 70s and the Gulf water warms to about 65 degrees. Rental pricing hits peak levels and availability gets tight, especially for the two main spring break weeks. If you are traveling with a family or group, book well in advance.

Outside of the spring break rush, March is a solid month. The weather is warming up, the fishing picks up for speckled trout, and the events calendar starts filling in. Texas SandFest, one of the largest sand sculpture competitions in the country, typically happens in late April but the lead-up events start drawing attention.

April

April is when Port Aransas starts to feel like summer. Highs reach the upper 70s and the Gulf water climbs to about 71 degrees, which is warm enough for comfortable swimming on sunny days. Crowds ease after spring break, and pricing drops from peak to high season rates.

This is one of the best months for fishing. The spring speckled trout run is in full swing, and offshore trips start producing king mackerel. On land, Texas SandFest and the Aransas Pass Shrimporee are two of the biggest events of the month. Sea turtle nesting season also begins in April, with Kemp's ridley females starting to come ashore on Mustang Island and Padre Island beaches.

May

May is the start of the sweet spot. Air temperatures hit the mid-80s, the Gulf water reaches 78 degrees, and the beaches are busy but not overwhelming. Pricing sits at the high-season level, which is more reasonable than the peak summer months. Memorial Day weekend is the exception, when crowds and rates spike for the holiday.

Fishing is excellent across the board. Speckled trout are still running strong, king mackerel are thick offshore, and the surf fishing picks up along the beachfront. Sea turtle nesting peaks in May and June, and if you are lucky you might see researchers working a nest on the beach.

June

June is peak summer in Port Aransas. Highs reach the upper 80s, the Gulf water hits 83 degrees, and the beaches are full. This is prime family vacation season and rental pricing is at its highest. The Deep Sea Roundup, one of the oldest fishing tournaments in Texas (running since 1932), happens in late June or early July and is a major community event.

Public sea turtle hatchling releases at Padre Island National Seashore typically start in mid-June. These free events are one of the most memorable wildlife experiences on the Texas coast. Call the hatchling alert line at 361-949-7163 for release announcements.

Tip: Check the Port Aransas water color and conditions forecast before your beach days. Water clarity varies with wind and currents, and a quick check helps you plan the best day for swimming or fishing.

July

July is the hottest month, with highs around 90 degrees and Gulf water at a bathtub-warm 85. Crowds remain at peak levels through the Fourth of July and stay high through the rest of the month. The fireworks show at Horace Caldwell Pier on the Fourth is one of the signature events of the summer.

This is the best month for swimming and water activities. The water is warm, the days are long, and the beach is in full summer mode. Dolphin-watching tours run daily from the marina, and golf cart rentals are the most popular way to get around town. Offshore fishing for king mackerel and red snapper (during federal season) is at its peak.

August

August is still hot, with highs reaching 91 degrees and the warmest Gulf water of the year at 86 degrees. Crowds start to thin slightly as school starts across Texas, and pricing drops from peak to high-season levels. This is a good window if you want warm water and beach weather without the full summer rush.

The main caution for August is the start of the more active portion of hurricane season. Tropical weather is always possible from June through November, but the peak risk months are August through October. Keep an eye on the forecast and have a flexible plan. Tarpon fishing picks up along the jetties in August and runs through October, offering some of the most exciting catch-and-release action on the coast.

September

September is one of the most underrated months to visit Port Aransas. The summer crowds are gone, highs are still in the upper 80s, and the Gulf water sits at a comfortable 84 degrees. Rental pricing drops to moderate levels, and you can often have long stretches of beach mostly to yourself.

This is one of the best months for fishing. The fall redfish run begins, with bull reds showing up along the jetties and beachfront. Offshore trips are productive for kingfish and the last of the summer species. September rainfall averages are the highest of the year at nearly six inches, so afternoon thunderstorms are common, but they usually pass quickly.

October

October is a favorite among locals and repeat visitors. Highs are in the low 80s, the water is still 78 degrees, and the weather is some of the calmest and most pleasant of the year. Crowds are moderate and pricing is reasonable.

The fall flounder run is the highlight for anglers. Flounder stack up in the passes and channels as they migrate toward the Gulf, and it is one of the most anticipated events on the fishing calendar. Bull redfish are still running strong along the beach. On the birding side, the whooping cranes begin returning to the Aransas National Wildlife Refuge in late October. The Texas Coastal Bend Wing Fest and Halloween events at Cinnamon Shore round out the month.

November

November brings cooler temperatures with highs in the low 70s and Gulf water around 70 degrees. Crowds are low and pricing is at off-season levels, making this one of the most affordable times to visit. The weather is mild and comfortable for outdoor activities, though a jacket is needed for evenings and early mornings.

Birding is excellent, with whooping cranes settled in at Aransas NWR and crane boat tours running from Rockport. Thanksgiving on the beach is a popular tradition for families who rent houses in Cinnamon Shore and cook a holiday meal with a Gulf view. Fishing remains productive for redfish and trout in the bays.

December

December is quiet and affordable. Highs average around 66 degrees and the Gulf water cools to about 63. This is not beach swimming weather, but it is perfect for long walks on the sand, fishing, and enjoying the small-town holiday atmosphere. The Cinnamon Shore Christmas Tree Lighting and Holiday Market is one of the highlights of the month, and New Year's Eve on the beach has become a growing tradition.

Rental pricing is at its lowest in December (outside of the holiday weeks), and you will find the most availability of any time of year. If you do not need warm water and want a peaceful, uncrowded coastal getaway, winter is hard to beat.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time of year to visit Port Aransas?
Late April through early June offers the best combination of warm water, manageable crowds, and moderate pricing. October and November are also excellent for calm weather, good fishing, and lower rates.

When is the Gulf water warm enough to swim?
Most people find the water comfortable from May (78 degrees) through October (78 degrees). July and August are the warmest at 85 to 86 degrees.

When is seaweed season in Port Aransas?
Sargassum seaweed is most common from May through August, with June and July typically being the heaviest months. The city cleans the public beaches regularly. Check the live beach conditions page before your trip.

When is hurricane season?
The Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 through November 30, with the peak risk months being August through October. Most years pass without a direct impact on Port Aransas, but it is smart to monitor forecasts and have a flexible travel plan during these months.

When is the cheapest time to visit?
December through February (excluding holiday weeks) offers the lowest rental rates and the most availability. September is also affordable and still has warm water.

What are the best months for fishing?
Fishing is good year-round, but the top months are April and May for speckled trout, September and October for redfish and flounder, and June through August for offshore species. See the full Port Aransas fishing guide for details.
