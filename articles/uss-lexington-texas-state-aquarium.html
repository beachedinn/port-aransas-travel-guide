USS Lexington and Texas State Aquarium

Two of the top attractions on the Texas Gulf Coast, both on North Beach in Corpus Christi

Home

Published on November 26, 2026

The USS Lexington Museum on the Bay and the Texas State Aquarium sit side by side on North Beach in Corpus Christi, about 40 minutes south of Port Aransas. Together they make one of the best day trips from Cinnamon Shore and Mustang Island, especially on days when the weather pushes you off the beach or when you want a break from the sand.

Both attractions can be done in a single day with time left over for lunch. Here is everything you need to know to plan your visit, from ticket prices and timing to what each venue actually delivers.

USS Lexington Museum on the Bay

The USS Lexington (CV-16) is a retired World War II aircraft carrier that served from 1943 through 1991. Nicknamed the "Blue Ghost" by Tokyo Rose after the Japanese reported sinking it multiple times, the carrier survived every attack and went on to serve longer than any other Essex-class carrier in U.S. Navy history. It has been moored in Corpus Christi Bay as a floating museum since 1992.

The museum offers five self-guided tour routes that take you through different sections of the ship. You can walk the flight deck to see vintage aircraft up close, visit the captain's bridge for views across the bay, explore the hangar bay where planes were stored and maintained, climb through the lower-deck crew quarters to see where thousands of sailors lived, and descend into the engine room that powered a vessel the length of three football fields.

Plan on spending about 2 to 3 hours aboard the Lexington. The ship is best suited for older kids and adults who can handle the ladders and narrow passageways. Young children may find the climbing tiring, and strollers are not practical on most of the tour routes.

Tip: The flight deck and hangar bay are accessible by elevator, but the lower decks require climbing metal ladders and navigating tight spaces. Wheelchairs and mobility devices can access the upper levels but not the crew quarters or engine room below.

USS Lexington Tickets

General admission is approximately $23 for adults and $15 for children. Military, senior, and AAA discounts are available. Check the museum's website for current pricing, since rates adjust seasonally.

Texas State Aquarium

The Texas State Aquarium is the largest aquarium in Texas and sits right next to the Lexington on North Beach. It focuses on the wildlife and ecosystems of the Gulf of Mexico and the Caribbean, with a mix of indoor exhibits and outdoor habitats that work for all ages.

The major exhibits include the Caribbean Sea tank with sharks and rays, the Dolphin Bay viewing area with underwater windows, a large sea otter habitat, and the H-E-B Splash Park where younger kids can cool off and play. The aquarium runs daily presentations and animal feedings throughout the day, so grab a schedule when you walk in and plan your route around the ones that interest you.

Plan on spending about 3 to 4 hours at the aquarium, more if you have young children who want time in the splash park. The facility is fully wheelchair accessible.

Texas State Aquarium Tickets

General admission is approximately $40 for adults and $30 for children. Children 2 and under are free.

AZA Reciprocal Membership: If you have an active membership from another participating zoo or aquarium in the Association of Zoos and Aquariums network, you may qualify for up to 50% off admission. Individual memberships get one discounted ticket; dual or family memberships get two. Bring your membership card and a photo ID. Some nearby institutions may be excluded from the program, so call ahead to confirm.

Combo Tickets: Both venues offer a combo ticket that covers admission to the USS Lexington and the aquarium at a discounted rate. You can buy combo tickets at either ticket window or online in advance, which is a good idea during peak season to skip the lines.

How to Do Both in One Day

Doing both the aquarium and the Lexington in a single day is very manageable. The two venues are within walking distance of each other on North Beach, and shared paid parking serves both. Pay once and walk between them.

The best approach is to start at the aquarium when it opens at 9 AM. The first hour or two before the crowds build after lunch is the quietest and most enjoyable time, especially with kids. After working through the exhibits, grab lunch, then walk over to the Lexington for the afternoon.

Total time for both venues runs about 6 to 8 hours, including a lunch break. If you have a half day, pick one. The aquarium is the better choice for families with young children and animal lovers. The Lexington is better for older kids, history buffs, and anyone interested in aviation or military history.

Planning tip: Weekday mornings are the quietest at both venues. Avoid Texas school holidays like spring break, the week of July 4th, fall break, Thanksgiving week, and Christmas break if you want a less crowded experience. Rainy days also draw bigger crowds since families look for indoor things to do off the beach.

Where to Eat

Both the aquarium and the Lexington have on-site cafes with standard options like burgers, sandwiches, and snacks. The food is decent but unremarkable. If you have an hour to spare between venues, downtown Corpus Christi is about 5 minutes away across the Harbor Bridge and has a wider selection of restaurants.

Another option is to pack a cooler and eat in the North Beach parking area. This works well for families with picky eaters and saves money on a day that already involves two sets of admission tickets. Getting There from Port Aransas

The drive from Port Aransas to North Beach in Corpus Christi takes about 40 minutes. Head south on TX-361, cross the JFK Causeway, then take TX-358 (South Padre Island Drive) west. Merge onto I-37 north and cross the Harbor Bridge (US-181), then take the North Beach exit. Both attractions are well-signed from there.

For more detailed route information, see the directions to Port Aransas guide, which covers the causeway and surrounding area.

Parking

North Beach has a shared paid parking lot that serves both the aquarium and the Lexington. Pay once and you are covered for the day. On quieter weekdays, you may also find free street parking along parts of North Beach Drive, but do not count on it during peak season or holidays.

Tips for Your Visit

Buy tickets online. During summer and holiday weekends, both venues can have long lines at the ticket windows. Buying online in advance saves time and sometimes offers a small discount.

Wear comfortable shoes. The Lexington involves a lot of walking on metal decks and climbing ladders. The aquarium has smoother surfaces but covers a large area. Sandals and flip-flops are not ideal for the carrier.

Bring sunscreen. The Lexington's flight deck is fully exposed with no shade. The aquarium's outdoor areas, including the splash park, are also in the sun. Apply sunscreen before you arrive.

Check the weather. Both venues are partly outdoors. The Lexington's flight deck and the aquarium's outdoor habitats and splash park are exposed. Light rain is manageable, but heavy storms can close outdoor sections. Check local conditions before you head out.

Bring cash for parking. While many lots accept cards, some street meters and smaller lots along North Beach are cash-only.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to visit both the USS Lexington and the aquarium?
Plan on about 6 to 8 hours total, including lunch. The aquarium takes 3 to 4 hours and the Lexington takes 2 to 3 hours.

Can I do both in one day?
Yes. Start at the aquarium at opening time, have lunch, then visit the Lexington in the afternoon. The two venues are within walking distance of each other.

Which one is better for young kids?
The aquarium. It has the splash park, animal feedings, and exhibits designed for younger visitors. The Lexington involves climbing metal ladders and navigating narrow spaces, which can be tiring and difficult for children under 7.

Is there a combo ticket?
Yes. Combo tickets covering both the USS Lexington and the Texas State Aquarium are available at either ticket window or online. The combo saves money compared to buying separately.

How far is North Beach from Port Aransas?
About 40 minutes by car. Take TX-361 south across the JFK Causeway, then follow SPID west and I-37 north over the Harbor Bridge to the North Beach exit.

Is the USS Lexington wheelchair accessible?
Partially. The flight deck and hangar bay are accessible by elevator, but the lower decks, crew quarters, and engine room require climbing metal ladders and are not accessible.

Is parking free?
Shared paid parking is available at North Beach. On quieter days, free street parking may be available along North Beach Drive, but it fills up during peak times.
