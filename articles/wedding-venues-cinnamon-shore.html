Wedding Venues at Cinnamon Shore

Beach, lawn, and village options for a Mustang Island wedding in Port Aransas

Cinnamon Shore on Mustang Island has become one of the more popular coastal wedding settings in Texas. The community offers Gulf beach ceremonies, inland lawn and pavilion spaces, a walkable village with restaurants for rehearsal dinners and welcome events, and vacation rentals that double as wedding-party lodging within walking distance of everything.

This guide covers what is available, how the booking process works, what to ask the events team, and how to handle vendor logistics and guest accommodations for a wedding at Cinnamon Shore in Port Aransas.

Venue Options at Cinnamon Shore

Wedding venues at Cinnamon Shore fall into four main categories, each with different strengths and trade-offs. All venue availability is managed through the Cinnamon Shore events team, so they should be your first call before booking any vendors.

Beach Ceremony on the Gulf

A ceremony on the beach is the main draw for most couples considering Cinnamon Shore. The wide, flat sand and boardwalk access from the community make setup relatively straightforward compared to other beach locations. Sunset and sunrise ceremonies photograph beautifully with the Gulf as a backdrop.

The trade-offs are real, though. Gulf wind picks up in the afternoon, which can wreak havoc on veils, decorations, and microphones. Morning or near-sunset timing is safer for wind. The tide line affects where chairs and the aisle can be placed, and beach ceremonies require a permit from the City of Port Aransas, which the events team coordinates. You will also want a rain backup plan locked in advance, since summer storm season on the Gulf Coast is unpredictable.

Tip: Talk to your photographer about sun direction before choosing a ceremony time. The angle of light at sunset versus morning makes a significant difference in how the photos turn out, and an experienced coastal photographer will have strong opinions about which works better for your specific setup.

Lawn or Pavilion Ceremony

Cinnamon Shore has lawn and pavilion spaces inside the community that offer a more controlled setting than the beach. Wind is less of a factor, vendor staging is easier, and the manicured grounds photograph well. These spaces work for small to medium guest counts, though capacity varies by specific location.

The trade-off is that you lose the open-sky, feet-in-the-sand feel that draws people to a beach wedding. For couples who love the idea of a coastal wedding but want more predictability, a lawn ceremony with the beach a short walk away for photos is a solid compromise.

Indoor or Lakehouse-Style Reception

For the reception, indoor and Lakehouse-style gathering spaces at Cinnamon Shore provide a weather-proof option for seated dinners and dancing. These spaces book up well in advance, especially during the spring and fall wedding seasons, so early planning is important.

An indoor reception paired with a beach ceremony gives you the best of both settings: the dramatic outdoor ceremony photos and a comfortable, climate-controlled space for the dinner and party afterward.

Village Restaurants for Welcome Events

The walkable village at Cinnamon Shore has several restaurants that work well for rehearsal dinners, welcome parties, and post-wedding brunches. Roosevelt's, Virginia's on the Bay, Lisabella's, and Castaways are among the options. For a broader look at dining on the island, see the Port Aransas restaurant guide.

Restaurant capacity is limited for large groups, so book tables of 6 or more at least 6 weeks ahead. For a more private option, the Cinnamon Shore concierge can connect you with private chefs who cook in-house at your rental, which works especially well for intimate rehearsal dinners.

How to Get Started

The first step is contacting the Cinnamon Shore events team. They control venue availability and can walk you through City of Port Aransas beach-permit requirements, available dates, and the community's approval process for vendors and setups.

Before you call, have these details ready:

  • Your target date range (a window of 2 to 3 weekends gives more flexibility)
  • Approximate guest count
  • Ceremony preference: beach, lawn, or indoor
  • Rough lodging headcount for the wedding party and out-of-town guests

Lock in the venue and date first. Vendors, lodging, and transportation all follow from there.

Vendor Logistics

Cinnamon Shore does not operate as an all-inclusive wedding venue. You will book vendors separately, which gives you flexibility but also means more coordination.

Finding vendors: The Cinnamon Shore events team and the Port Aransas Tourism Bureau both maintain vendor lists. Local planners with Cinnamon Shore experience are worth considering because they understand the community's approval processes and logistical quirks.

Booking lead times: Photography sunset slots during spring and fall wedding season book 6 to 9 months or more in advance. Catering for groups over 50 should be locked at least 4 months ahead. The earlier you start, the more options you have.

Vendor travel: Many vendors are based in Corpus Christi, which is about 35 to 45 minutes away via the JFK Causeway. Build that travel time into your day-of timeline so vendors arrive with time to set up without rushing. For more on the route, see the directions to Port Aransas guide.

Planning tip: If you are hiring a wedding planner, choose one with specific Cinnamon Shore experience. The community has its own processes for vendor access, setup, and teardown that a planner familiar with the property can navigate much more smoothly than someone working the location for the first time.

Beach Ceremony Considerations

Beach weddings on the Gulf Coast are beautiful but require more planning than an indoor ceremony. Here is what to think through:

Wind. The Gulf breeze is constant and picks up in the afternoon. Schedule the ceremony for morning or near sunset when wind is typically calmer. Avoid lightweight decorations that can blow away, and consider a clip or pins for the veil.

Sun direction. Your photographer should weigh in on timing based on where the sun will be. Harsh midday light is unflattering. Golden hour near sunset is ideal but leaves a narrow window.

Tide. The high-tide line determines how much usable beach you have for chairs and an aisle. Check tide charts for your date and plan the layout accordingly.

Permits. Beach ceremonies require a permit from the City of Port Aransas. The Cinnamon Shore events team handles the coordination, but make sure this is confirmed well before the wedding date.

Rain backup. Set a lawn or indoor backup before the wedding day. Having a Plan B already locked means a last-minute weather change is a logistics adjustment, not a crisis. Gulf Coast storms can roll in fast during summer months. Check the beach conditions page as the date approaches.

Wedding-Party Lodging

One of the biggest advantages of a Cinnamon Shore wedding is that the wedding party and guests can stay in vacation rentals within the same community, all within walking or golf-cart distance of the ceremony and reception. No shuttles, no designated drivers, no one getting lost trying to find a hotel 20 minutes away.

A typical setup involves 3 to 6 homes: a bridal-party home for the bride and bridesmaids, a separate home for the groom and groomsmen, homes for immediate family, and additional homes for out-of-town guests. Cinnamon Shore Vacations can coordinate group blocks so your guests are clustered together.

Beached Inn is a 3-bedroom vacation rental inside Cinnamon Shore that sleeps up to 10. It works well as a bridal-party home because the private elevator moves the dress, photography gear, and coolers between floors without anyone navigating stairs. The vaulted great room and covered porch are natural spots for getting-ready photos and pre-ceremony staging. The house sits about a two-minute walk from the beach and is walkable to the village restaurants. Book directly at beachedinn.com.

Best Months for a Cinnamon Shore Wedding

The best wedding months at Cinnamon Shore are April through early June and September through October. These shoulder seasons offer milder temperatures, lower humidity, smaller crowds, and better pricing on both venues and lodging compared to peak summer.

Avoid mid-March if you do not want to compete with spring break crowds. August and September bring the peak of Gulf Coast storm season, so if you are considering a September wedding, have a solid rain backup plan and be prepared for the possibility of tropical weather. For a full month-by-month breakdown, see the best time to visit Port Aransas guide.

Budget Planning

Cinnamon Shore does not offer an all-inclusive wedding package. The venue, vendors, and lodging are all booked and paid separately, which means you have full control over where the money goes but also need to track more moving pieces.

Small beach ceremonies with under 50 guests can cost significantly less than a traditional Texas resort wedding. Weddings of 100 or more guests with a full reception, multi-night lodging block, and complete vendor team can approach resort-level budgets.

Request itemized quotes for venue fees, vendor packages, lodging blocks, and beach-permit and rental costs. Shoulder-season pricing applies to both venues and rentals, so an April or October wedding can save meaningfully compared to a July date.

Getting There

For out-of-town guests flying in, the closest airport is Corpus Christi International (CRP), about 35 to 45 minutes from Cinnamon Shore via the JFK Causeway. San Antonio International (SAT) is about 2 hours and 45 minutes away, and Austin-Bergstrom (AUS) is about 3 hours and 45 minutes. Include airport and driving details in your wedding communications so guests can plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you get married at Cinnamon Shore?
Yes. The community offers beach ceremonies on the Gulf, lawn and pavilion spaces inside the community, and indoor reception venues. All bookings go through the Cinnamon Shore events team.

How do I book a wedding venue at Cinnamon Shore?
Contact the Cinnamon Shore events team first. They manage venue availability, coordinate beach permits with the City of Port Aransas, and can advise on dates, capacity, and vendor logistics.

What is the best month for a Cinnamon Shore wedding?
April through early June and September through October. These months have milder weather, smaller crowds, and better pricing. Avoid mid-March spring break and be cautious with late August and September storm season.

Where does the wedding party stay?
In vacation rentals inside Cinnamon Shore. A typical wedding books 3 to 6 homes for the bridal party, groom's party, family, and guests. Everything is within walking or golf-cart distance.

Is the beach good for ceremonies?
Yes. The beach at Cinnamon Shore is wide and flat with boardwalk access from the community. Plan around afternoon wind by scheduling a morning or sunset ceremony, and always have an indoor or lawn backup ready.

What is the closest airport for wedding guests?
Corpus Christi International (CRP), about 35 to 45 minutes away. San Antonio (SAT) and Austin (AUS) are also options with more airline choices. See the airport guide for details.

Where should we have the rehearsal dinner?
The walkable village at Cinnamon Shore has several restaurants, including Roosevelt's, Virginia's on the Bay, Lisabella's, and Castaways. For a more private option, hire a private chef to cook in-house at your rental. Book large-group restaurant tables at least 6 weeks ahead.

Do we need a wedding planner?
It is strongly recommended, especially one with Cinnamon Shore experience. The community has its own vendor approval processes and logistical requirements that a local planner can navigate efficiently.
