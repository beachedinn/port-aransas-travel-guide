Directions to Port Aransas

Every route, both crossings, and what to know before you drive

Home

Published on November 9, 2026

Getting to Port Aransas is straightforward once you know the two ways onto the island. One involves a free ferry. The other skips it entirely. Which route you take depends on where you are coming from and how you feel about waiting in line.

Here is a breakdown of every major route, both island crossings, the airport situation, and a few things that will make your arrival day smoother.

Two Ways Onto the Island

Port Aransas sits on Mustang Island, which means you need to cross water to get there. You have two options, and understanding them is the single most useful thing you can know before your trip.

Option 1: The JFK Causeway (No Ferry)

This is the route most experienced visitors prefer, especially on busy weekends. From Corpus Christi, take TX-358 (South Padre Island Drive) east across the JFK Causeway onto North Padre Island. Then head north on TX-361 up Mustang Island directly into Port Aransas.

The drive from downtown Corpus Christi to Port Aransas via this route is about 38 miles and takes roughly 48 minutes in normal traffic. There is no ferry, no line, and no waiting. You cross a bridge, drive up the island, and you are there.

This is the route to take if you are arriving on a Friday afternoon, during a holiday weekend, or anytime the ferry might have a long queue. It is also the better option if you are coming from the south or west, since you are already passing through or near Corpus Christi.

Option 2: The Aransas Pass Ferry

The free Port Aransas ferry runs from Aransas Pass across the ship channel to the north end of Mustang Island. It operates 24 hours a day, seven days a week, weather permitting. The crossing itself takes under ten minutes, and dolphins are regularly spotted alongside the boats.

Each ferry carries up to 20 vehicles, with two to six ferries running at any given time depending on demand. On a quiet Tuesday morning, you might drive right on. On a Saturday afternoon in July, you could wait 45 minutes or more.

The ferry is the more direct route if you are coming from the north (Houston, Austin, or Dallas via US-181 through Aransas Pass). It is also an experience in itself, especially for kids and first-time visitors. Just factor in potential wait times when planning your arrival.

You can check the ferry status and landing cameras on the TxDOT website before you head out.

Drive Times from Major Texas Cities

All estimates below use the JFK Causeway route through Corpus Christi, which avoids the ferry and gives you a more predictable arrival time. Actual drive times will vary with traffic and conditions.

From San Antonio: About 3 hours. Take I-37 south to Corpus Christi, then TX-358 east across the JFK Causeway to Mustang Island. This is the most common route for weekend visitors, and Port Aransas is one of the closest beaches to San Antonio.

From Austin: About 3.5 hours. Take I-35 south to San Antonio, then I-37 south to Corpus Christi, and follow the causeway route from there. The drive is mostly highway and moves well outside of San Antonio rush hour.

From Houston: About 4 hours. Take US-59 south to Victoria, then US-77 south toward Corpus Christi, picking up TX-358 to the causeway. Alternatively, you can take US-181 through Aransas Pass and ride the ferry, which is slightly shorter in distance but adds ferry wait time.

From Dallas/Fort Worth: About 6 hours. Take I-35 south through Austin and San Antonio to I-37, then follow the Corpus Christi route. This is a full day of driving, so most DFW visitors either leave early in the morning or break the drive with a stop in San Antonio.

Flying In

The closest commercial airport to Port Aransas is Corpus Christi International Airport (CRP), about 41 miles and 45 minutes from the island via the JFK Causeway route. CRP has direct flights from several major Texas cities and connecting service through Dallas, Houston, and Denver.

San Antonio International Airport (SAT) and Austin-Bergstrom International Airport (AUS) are alternatives with more flight options and often better fares, especially for visitors flying in from out of state. The tradeoff is a longer drive to the coast: about 3 hours from San Antonio and 3.5 from Austin.

There is also Mustang Beach Airport (KRAS) in Port Aransas, but it serves general aviation only. No scheduled commercial flights operate from there.

You will need a rental car regardless of which airport you fly into. There is no public transit to Port Aransas, and rideshare availability on the island is limited.

Step by Step: The Causeway Route

For first-time visitors, here is the JFK Causeway route broken down into simple steps.

1. Get to Corpus Christi using whatever highway brings you in from your starting city. I-37 is the main corridor from San Antonio. US-77 and US-181 serve traffic from the north and east.

2. In Corpus Christi, follow signs for TX-358 East (South Padre Island Drive). This takes you across the JFK Causeway, a long bridge over the Laguna Madre, onto North Padre Island.

3. Once on North Padre Island, turn left (north) onto TX-361 (Park Road 22). This road runs up Mustang Island directly into Port Aransas. The drive up the island is about 20 minutes.

4. Follow TX-361 into town. For the Cinnamon Shore community, your GPS will guide you to the specific address. For downtown Port Aransas, continue north on TX-361 until it becomes Alister Street.

Tips for Arrival Day

Stop for groceries on the way in. The H-E-B in Portland (just north of Corpus Christi on US-181) or the stores in Corpus Christi are your best options for a full grocery run. Port Aransas has a small IGA market, but selection is limited and prices are higher. Stock up before you cross onto the island.

Rent a golf cart in advance. Most rental companies deliver the cart to your vacation rental property. Once you have a golf cart, you can park your car and not touch it again until checkout. Golf carts are the primary way people get around Port Aransas, and they fill up fast during peak weekends.

Check the ferry before you commit to it. If you are coming from the north and plan to take the ferry, check the TxDOT ferry status page or the landing cameras before you get in line. If the wait looks long, you can reroute through Corpus Christi and take the causeway instead. It adds about 30 minutes of driving but eliminates the wait.

Arrive before dark if you can. Port Aransas is not a well-lit city, and finding your rental property for the first time is easier in daylight. Most vacation rentals use self-check-in with door codes, so there is no one meeting you at the door.

Fill up your gas tank before crossing onto the island. There are gas stations in Port Aransas, but fuel prices on the island tend to be higher than on the mainland. Top off in Aransas Pass or Corpus Christi.
